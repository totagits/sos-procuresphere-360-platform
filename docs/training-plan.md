# Training Plan

## Audience
- Procurement team
- Finance team
- Programme approvers
- Warehouse and receiving users
- Internal audit
- System administrators

## Training Format
- Administrator configuration session
- End-user workflow labs
- UAT-supported practice scenarios
- Quick-reference guides and recorded walkthroughs

## Proposed Delivery
- Day 1: Admin and super-user configuration workshop
- Day 2: Procurement and supplier workflow hands-on training
- Day 3: Finance, matching, payment, DMS, and audit pack workshop
- Day 4: UAT-assisted live practice and remediation

## Materials To Deliver
- Slide deck
- Quick guides
- Scenario scripts
- Attendance and competency checklist
- Video tutorial recordings
